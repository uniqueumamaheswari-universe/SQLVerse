## 📌 Purpose

## 🍰 The Opening

**Mathematics without set theory is like a dessert without sugar.**

Before we ask what a relationship is mathematically, we should pause and acknowledge the mathematical language in which relationships can be expressed.

A relational database is not merely a collection of tables.

At its mathematical foundation are **sets**, **elements**, **relations**, **mappings**, **products**, and **operations over collections of values**.

SQL gives us a practical language for manipulating these structures.  
Set theory gives us a language for understanding them.

*This document builds mathematical vocabulary progressively. Nothing is hidden; nothing is revealed before it is earned.*

## 🧮 Why Mathematics Comes First

The Grain Triad taught the reader to reason about data model transformations. `03C-grain-joins-mathematics.md` extracts the mathematics from those transformations.

But the extraction requires vocabulary the reader may not yet have.

The mathematical vocabulary required by 03C-grain-joins-mathematics.md — sets, subsets, membership, Cartesian products, relations — was originally introduced inline as the file progressed. Separating it into this prerequisite preserves 03C-grain-joins-mathematics.md's discovery arc intact, and gives the vocabulary its own continuous arc.

This folder separates the vocabulary from the discovery. It lets the reader acquire the mathematical language in one continuous arc — and then read `03C-grain-joins-mathematics.md` in a second, unbroken arc.

> **Don't hide mathematics. Don't reveal it prematurely either.**

The prerequisite builds vocabulary progressively. Nothing is hidden; nothing is revealed before it is earned.
